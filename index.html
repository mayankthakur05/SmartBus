How It Works

Smart Route Matching & Time Window Logic

SmartBus goes beyond naive start-to-end matching by evaluating the entire ordered sequence and intermediate schedules of every trip.

1

Select Any Two Stops

Choose any boarding and drop-off points along a corridor. Both can be intermediate stops (e.g. searching LPU to Goraya on a Ludhiana to Batala trip).

2

1-Hour Departure Window

When a preferred time (e.g. 3:00 PM) is set, SmartBus checks the bus's scheduled time at your selected boarding stop, including departures between 3:00 PM and 4:00 PM.

3

Fastest Journey Highlight

Eligible buses are evaluated for minimum journey duration (arr_time - dep_time). The fastest option is prominently highlighted with a special badge.

Academic Architecture

How SmartBus Recommends Buses

This project uses a transparent, deterministic rule-based ranking algorithm. There are no fabricated AI percentages, simulated traffic delays, or black-box predictions.

Ranking Hierarchy & Criteria

Priority 1

Route Direction & Selected Departure Window

Guarantees the trip visits origin before destination (seq_src < seq_dst) and departs within $[T, T+60\text{ min}]$ from the selected boarding station.

Priority 2

Fastest Journey Recommendation

Identifies the shortest calculated travel duration between requested stops. If multiple buses share the minimum time, they are marked as tied fastest options.

Priority 3

Fewer Intermediate Stops

Express buses with fewer stops between your chosen locations are prioritized over local buses that stop at every intermediate station.

Priority 4

Lower Fare When Available

Exact ticket fares looked up from the fare matrix for the specific origin, destination, and bus comfort category.

Priority 5

Passenger Bus Type Preference

Directly respects user filter selection (AC vs Non-AC) and deterministically breaks ties by scheduled departure time.

Academic Integrity Note

Many student projects simulate "AI recommendations" by generating random percentages or hardcoding arbitrary scores. SmartBus enforces academic integrity:

  • Real MongoDB Data: Queries actual documents across all 6 collections.
  • Origin Stop Boarding Time: Uses the vehicle's arrival at the passenger's boarding stop, not the trip's starting depot time.
  • Safe Duration Math: Erroneous midday backwards timetables are marked "Duration unavailable" and excluded from fastest badges.
  • Overnight Support: Spans midnight correctly (e.g. 11:30 PM to 1:45 AM).
Core Capabilities

Engineered for Real-World Transit Queries

Key features designed for passenger convenience and system reliability.

1-Hour Departure Window

Filter buses departing your specific stop within an hour of your preferred travel time, handling 12h/24h formats and midnight wrap-around.

Fastest Bus Recommendation

Dynamically compares segment durations between requested stations, highlighting the fastest choice and supporting tied fastest options.

Intermediate Stop Search

Search between any two transit stops on an intercity route without requiring the trip's starting terminal or final depot.

Exact Fare Matrix Lookup

Retrieves verified ticket prices mapped directly to your travel segment and bus comfort category.

Journey-Specific Route Timeline

Interactive route viewer displaying only the stops relevant to your journey, omitting stops before boarding or after drop-off.

Dynamic Multi-Criteria Sorting

Switch seamlessly between Recommended, Fastest Journey, Fewest Stops, or Lowest Fare with instant live updates.

Notice: Scheduled vs Live Information

SmartBus operates using static published intercity timetables stored in MongoDB. The application does not simulate GPS vehicle tracking or live road traffic conditions. Departure and arrival times represent published transport schedules for Punjab intercity corridors.
